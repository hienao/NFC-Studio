## 1.1.14 — submitted for review / 已提交审核

Submitted on 2026-10-28. Public availability is not yet confirmed. / 2026 年 10 月 28 日提交，尚未确认公开上线。

- Adds an annual Pro subscription alongside lifetime purchase, with access to Pro tools and no banner ads while active.
- Annual subscriptions renew automatically; manage or cancel through Google Play. Available plans and local prices appear before purchase.
- Improves purchase restoration and subscription status refresh.
- 在永久买断之外新增 Pro 年度订阅，有效期内提供全部 Pro 工具并移除横幅广告。
- 年度订阅自动续费，可在 Google Play 管理或取消，可用方案与当地价格在购买前显示。
- 完善购买恢复与订阅状态刷新。
